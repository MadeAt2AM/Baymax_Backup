# Email Drafts — Singapore Aircon Cold Outreach

**Sender:** Chris (via madeat2am.ai@gmail.com)
**Voice:** Genuinely curious, conversational, low-pressure. Not pitching, not selling — just asking a question.
**Goal of the email:** Start a conversation, not close a sale. The recipient should feel comfortable replying.
**Personalisation:** Each draft pulls the recipient's company name + area in Singapore into the first paragraph so it doesn't look like a mass blast.

---

## Shared Subject Lines (3 variants — pick one)

**Option A — Direct question:**
> Quick question about [Company Name]'s online presence

**Option B — Curiosity-led:**
> Did [Company Name] used to have a website?

**Option C — Observation-led:**
> Found your [Company Name] listing on Google

I'd recommend **Option A** for the first wave — it's the most natural, matches the body copy, and reads like a real person who genuinely has a question.

## Email Template (Master Copy)

```
Hi [First Name / Team],

I was searching Google for aircon service companies in [Area] and your
business [Company Name] came up — I tried to find a website for you
but couldn't track one down, so I figured I'd write and ask.

Do you have a website at the moment? If yes, I'd love the link — happy
to be pointed in the right direction. If not, is that something on
your radar at all, or are you mostly getting work through word of
mouth and the phone?

No pitch in this email. I'm asking because I work with a handful of
Singapore service businesses on their online presence and I'm trying
to understand how the industry handles it — what's working, what's
not.

If you're open to it, I'd appreciate a one-line reply either way.

Cheers,
Chris
MadeAt2Am
```
