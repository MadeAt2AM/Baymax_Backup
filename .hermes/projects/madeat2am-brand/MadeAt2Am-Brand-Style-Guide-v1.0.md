# MadeAt2Am — Brand Style Guide
**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** June 2026
**Line Spacing:** 1.25

## 1. Brand Persona & Philosophy

The name MadeAt2Am evokes raw creation, late-night obsession, and the unfiltered clarity that arrives when the rest of the world is asleep. It is aggressive, sharp, and deeply focused. To prevent this raw energy from looking amateur, the visual identity uses a strict color hierarchy to anchor its intense accents with premium structural grounding.

---

## 2. The Color Matrix

The brand utilizes a four-color system engineered for maximum contrast and accessibility. The distribution must strictly follow the **60-30-10 rule** to avoid a chaotic visual profile.

| Color Name       | Hex Code  | Target Distribution | Strategic Function                    |
|------------------|-----------|---------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Warm Cream       | `#FDFBF7` | 60% (Canvas)        | Dominant background. Softens the starkness of pure white; delivers an editorial, premium aesthetic. |
| Obsidian Black   | `#111111` | 30% (Structure)     | Primary text, layout borders, and dark-mode surfaces. Anchors the design with stark authority. |
| Crimson Red      | `#D32F2F` | 8% (Action)         | Primary CTA elements, urgency highlights, and critical branding assets. |
| Cyber Yellow     | `#FFC107` | 2% (Spotlight)      | Micro-interactions, text highlights, alert banners, and precise visual focal points. |

---

## 3. Digital Accessibility & Contrast Rules

To comply with WCAG 2.1 contrast standards and maintain a premium appearance, implementation teams must follow these strict combination parameters:

### Approved High-Contrast Pairings (Mandatory)

- **Body Copy:** Obsidian Black (`#111111`) text overlaying Warm Cream (`#FDFBF7`) background.
- **Dark Mode / Hero Sections:** Warm Cream (`#FDFBF7`) text overlaying Obsidian Black (`#111111`) background.
- **High-Impact Highlights:** Obsidian Black (`#111111`) text overlaying a Cyber Yellow (`#FFC107`) element or background badge.
- **Primary CTAs:** Warm Cream (`#FDFBF7`) text centered on a Crimson Red (`#D32F2F`) button container.

### Prohibited Pairings (Fails Standards)

- ❌ Crimson Red on Obsidian Black (or vice versa): Causes severe chromatic aberration and legibility strain on digital screens.
- ❌ Cyber Yellow on Warm Cream: Insufficient contrast ratio; content becomes completely invisible.
- ❌ Crimson Red text on Cyber Yellow background: Creates a cheap, retail clearance visual identity. Avoid entirely.

---

## 4. Typography

Layout To preserve the clean editorial structure, typography must use standard, high-legibility digital sans-serif typefaces (such as Arial) with explicit spacing hierarchy.

| Element        | Style                                    | Usage |
|----------------|------------------------------------------|-------|
| H1 Headings    | Obsidian Black, Bold, heavy optical weight. Used sparingly. | Reserved for major titles and hero text only. |
| Body Copy      | Obsidian Black on Warm Cream canvas, tracking at default, line-spacing set to **1.25** for comfortable readability. | All standard body text, descriptions, captions. |
| Micro-Labels   | Cyber Yellow background badge with Obsidian Black uppercase text. | Tags, statuses, categories. |

---

## 5. Application Checklist

- [ ] Is Warm Cream the primary background for at least 60% of the viewport?
- [ ] Are the red elements reserved strictly for interactive actions or core branding markers?
- [ ] Has all body text maintained an off-black hue (`#111111`) to maximize scanning comfort?
- [ ] Are yellow elements localized to precise highlights rather than broad structural blocks?
