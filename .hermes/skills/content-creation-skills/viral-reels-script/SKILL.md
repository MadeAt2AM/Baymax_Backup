# Viral Reels Script Skill

A complete system for writing scroll-stopping, high-retention short-form video scripts across any niche.

---

## Step 1 — Gather Context First

Before writing, clarify these if not already provided:

| Info Needed | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| **Topic / Niche** | Scripts must be niche-specific to trigger the right audience |
| **Target Audience** | Who's watching (beginners, experts, specific demographics) |
| **Goal** | Followers, sales, engagement, authority, or awareness |
| **Tone** | Educational, inspirational, funny, raw/authentic, authoritative |
| **Length** | 15s, 30s, 60s, or 90s — changes script density |
| **Platform** | Reels, TikTok, Shorts — subtle format differences apply |

If the user gives a topic without other details, make reasonable assumptions and state them clearly. Do not ask multiple questions at once — ask one, or just proceed with stated assumptions.

---

## Step 2 — Choose a Hook Style

The hook is everything. **71% of viewers decide within the first 3 seconds** whether to keep watching. Pick one hook archetype and execute it with specificity.

### The 10 Core Hook Archetypes

**1. Curiosity Gap** — Open a loop, delay the payoff
> "Nobody talks about this when starting a business…"
> "The one Instagram trick nobody is using yet."

**2. Pain Point Interrupt** — Name their exact struggle
> "This is why your Reels are stuck under 500 views."
> "You're posting Reels wrong. Here's why."

**3. Scroll-Stop Command** — Direct address, pattern break
> "Stop scrolling if you want to grow on Instagram in 2026."
> "Before you post another Reel, watch this."

**4. Transformation Promise** — Clear before → after
> "How I went from 0 to 10K followers in 90 days."
> "I did this for 30 days. Here's what happened."

**5. Contrarian / Myth-Bust** — Challenge conventional wisdom
> "The brutal truth about going viral on Instagram."
> "Every guru is wrong about the algorithm. Here's proof."

**6. Regret Hook** — Relatability + authority
> "I wish I knew this before posting my first 100 Reels."
> "If I had to start Instagram from scratch in 2026, I'd do this."

**7. Specificity / Number Hook** — Lists and data are scannable
> "3 mistakes killing your Instagram Reels growth right now."
> "I tested 47 hooks. Only 3 actually worked."

**8. Identity / POV Hook** — Mirror the viewer's identity
> "POV: You finally understand how the Instagram algorithm works."
> "If you're a creator who feels invisible on Instagram, this is for you."

**9. Steal / Give Hook** — Immediate value transfer
> "Steal this viral Instagram Reels script."
> "Here's exactly what to say in your next Reel."

**10. High Stakes / Exclusive** — FOMO and urgency
> "The 2026 strategy nobody is using yet…"
> "This will change how you create content forever."

### Hook Quality Checklist
- [ ] Delivers the core promise in under 5 words of on-screen text
- [ ] Creates a "curiosity gap" or names a pain point
- [ ] Avoids vague intros ("Hey guys, today I want to…")
- [ ] Passes the **click confirmation test**: the hook matches what the video actually delivers
- [ ] Uses a specific number, timeframe, or result where possible

## Step 3 — Build the Full Script

Use this **4-Part Viral Script Structure**:

### Part 1: Hook (0–3 seconds)
One sentence. Maximum impact. See Step 2.

### Part 2: Expansion / Open Loop (3–10 seconds)
Validate the hook. Expand the promise. Tease what's coming without giving it away yet.
> "Most creators skip this step — and it's the reason their views cap out every time."

Use the **Expectation → Exceed** principle: your first few sentences should confirm, then slightly beat, the promise your hook made.

### Part 3: Value Body (10–60 seconds, depending on length)
Deliver the core content. Structure options:

- **List Format**: 3 tips / mistakes / steps (each ~5 seconds)
- **Storytelling Arc**: Struggle → Failure → Unexpected Lesson
- **Tutorial**: Problem → Solution → Proof it works
- **Comparison**: Wrong way → Right way (split-screen friendly)

**Retention Rules for the Body:**
- Cut every sentence that doesn't add tension or clarity
- Use **mini-hooks** between points: *"That was important — but what I'm about to say next is what most people miss."*
- Include a visual cue or B-roll suggestion every 5–7 seconds
- Speak to one person, not a crowd ("you" not "people")
- Use the word "because" to explain, not just state

### Part 4: CTA (Last 3–5 seconds)
Never skip this. One CTA only. Match it to your goal:

| Goal | CTA |
|---|---|
| Followers | "Follow for more [niche] tips every week." |
| Saves | "Save this so you don't forget." |
| Comments | "Comment '[WORD]' and I'll send you the full breakdown." |
| Part 2 | "Comment 'MORE' if you want part 2." |
| Sales | "Link in bio for the full [course/guide/template]." |

## Step 4 — Apply Retention Psychology

Every word in a viral script serves one of three functions:

1. **Hook** — stops the scroll
2. **Retain** — keeps the viewer watching
3. **Reward** — delivers value that makes them save/share

### The Hormozi Framework (Hook → Retain → Reward)
- **Hook**: Create intrigue immediately. One core promise.
- **Retain**: Open loops, mini-hooks between points, pacing variation
- **Reward**: Over-deliver on the promise. Give more than expected.

### The MrBeast Principle
- Front-load excitement. The first 10 seconds must validate the hook.
- Eliminate dead air. Every moment must add tension or clarity.
- Re-engage after every point with a mini-hook before the next.
- The outro is not an afterthought — it seals the watch-through and drives action.

### Authenticity Over Production (2026 Reality)
Authentic, raw content now outperforms polished productions. Viewers want transparency. Do not write scripts that sound corporate or overly produced. Write like the creator is talking to one specific person.

## Step 6 — Length-Specific Guidance

| Length | Word Count | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| **15 seconds** | ~40–50 words | Hook + 1 insight + CTA only |
| **30 seconds** | ~75–90 words | Hook + Expand + 2 points + CTA |
| **60 seconds** | ~150–180 words | Full 4-part structure, 3 points |
| **90 seconds** | ~200–240 words | Full structure + storytelling arc or 4–5 points |

Shorter = higher completion rate = better algorithm signals. Default to 30–60 seconds unless the user specifies otherwise.
