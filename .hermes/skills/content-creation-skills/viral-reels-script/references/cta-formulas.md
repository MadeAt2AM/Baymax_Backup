# CTA Formulas — 30+ Options by Goal

Always use one CTA only. Match it to the creator's primary goal.

---

## FOLLOW CTAs (Goal: Grow Audience)
1. "Follow for one [niche] tip every day that actually works."
2. "Follow if you want more [niche] content that nobody else is sharing."
3. "Hit follow — I post [frequency] and every video will save you [time/money/effort]."
4. "If this was helpful, follow — there's a lot more where that came from."
5. "Follow for part 2 — it's even better than this."

---

## SAVE CTAs (Goal: Algorithm Boost + Return Viewers)
6. "Save this so you don't forget — you'll want to come back to it."
7. "Save this before you post your next [Reel / recipe / workout]."
8. "Screenshot this and save it — it's worth having in your camera roll."
9. "Save this and try it this week. Then come back and tell me how it went."
10. "This took me [X months] to learn. Save it so you don't have to."

---

## COMMENT CTAs (Goal: Engagement + Reach)
11. "Comment '[WORD]' and I'll send you the full breakdown / template / guide."
12. "Comment your [niche / situation] below — I'll reply with a personalized tip."
13. "Drop a [emoji] if this hit different."
14. "Tell me in the comments — which one are you guilty of?"
15. "Comment 'MORE' if you want part 2."
16. "What would you add? Drop it below."
17. "Comment your biggest struggle with [topic] — I'm reading every one."

---

## SHARE CTAs (Goal: Virality + Reach)
18. "Send this to a friend who needs to hear it."
19. "Share this with someone who's still [doing the wrong thing]."
20. "Tag someone who needs to see this."
21. "This is the kind of content worth sharing — pass it on."

---

## SALES / LINK CTAs (Goal: Conversion)
22. "Link in bio for the full [course / guide / template / system]."
23. "DM me '[WORD]' and I'll send you the free [resource]."
24. "Want the full breakdown? It's in the link in bio."
25. "I made a free [guide / checklist / template] for this — link in bio."
26. "If you want to go deeper on this, check the link in my bio."

---

## COMMUNITY / SERIES CTAs (Goal: Loyalty + Return Views)
27. "Follow so you don't miss the next one — this is a series."
28. "I'm posting every [day/week] about [topic]. Follow and turn on notifications."
29. "If you're doing this challenge with me — comment 'IN' below."
30. "This is week [X] of my [30-day / 90-day] series. Follow to see what happens."

---

## POWER CTA RULES

**Rule 1 — One CTA only.** Giving multiple actions reduces all of them.

**Rule 2 — Make it frictionless.** The CTA should require one tap or one word. The more effort required, the fewer people complete it.

**Rule 3 — Match the ask to the value delivered.** Big value → ask for a follow or share. Small tip → ask for a like or save.

**Rule 4 — Embed the CTA into the content.** Don't tack it on at the end like an afterthought. Transition into it naturally.

**Example of a weak CTA:**
> "If you enjoyed this video, please like, comment, subscribe, and share it with your friends!"

**Example of a strong CTA:**
> "Save this before your next workout — and comment what you're training this week."

**Rule 5 — The 'Comment X and I'll send you Y' formula is one of the highest-performing CTAs in 2026.** It generates comments (algorithm signal) + creates a DM automation opportunity + builds 1:1 audience connection.
