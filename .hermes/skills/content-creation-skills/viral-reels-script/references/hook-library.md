## HOOK FORMULA (Build Your Own)

Use this template to generate custom hooks:

**[Emotion Trigger] + [Specific Audience Signal] + [Curiosity / Pain / Promise]**

Examples:
- *"Nobody (curiosity) talks about this when you're a new coach (audience) starting on Instagram (context)…"*
- *"Stop (command) scrolling if you're a fitness creator (audience) who wants more saves (promise)."*
- *"The brutal truth (emotion) about going viral (topic) that nobody will say out loud (exclusivity)."*

## On-Screen Text Tips

The visual hook (text overlay) and spoken hook work together:

- Spoken hook = full sentence, conversational
- On-screen text = 3–6 words, punchy, bold
- They should complement, NOT repeat each other

**Example:**
- Spoken: *"Nobody talks about this when you're trying to grow on Instagram..."*
- On-screen: **"THE REEL MISTAKE KILLING YOUR GROWTH"**
